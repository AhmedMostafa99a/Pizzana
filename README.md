# Pizzana

## Requesting Orders
To request an order from Pizzana, please follow these steps:
1. Run the application.
2. Enter the id of the meal you wish to order when prompted.
3. Add extras to the meal by entering their ids when prompted and just enter -1 to finish adding extras.
4. You will receive a summary of your order including the meals, discounts and total price.
5. Choose your payment method when prompted (cash, mobile wallet or credit card).
6. If you choose credit card or mobile wallet, enter your details when prompted.
7. Confirm your order to complete the process.
8. You will receive an order confirmation message.
9. Enjoy your meal!
